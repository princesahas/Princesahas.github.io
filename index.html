I will continue updating this website as I gain new skills, complete projects, participate in competitions and gain experience.

Profiles of Successful Students

I studied student profiles from premier institutes to understand different paths involving academics, projects, research, competitions, internships and leadership.

1. Ray Wang — MIT

Ray Wang is an MIT student studying Artificial Intelligence and Decision Making and Physics. He has been involved in MIT's Undergraduate Research Opportunities Program (UROP) since Fall 2022.

His research experience includes computer vision, generative modeling and medical imaging. He also worked on a method related to large language model fine-tuning and later worked on improving image generation quality.

One important part of his journey is that an idea developed during his research eventually became a paper.

What I learned: Long-term research and curiosity can turn an idea into meaningful technical work. Students should explore different areas before deciding what they want to specialize in.

2. Cole Firlie — MIT

Cole Firlie is an MIT student studying Computer Science and Molecular Biology.

Through UROP, he has worked on different technically challenging areas including machine-learning flight control for quadcopters, reactor optics and astronaut physiology systems.

His profile shows how combining knowledge from different fields can create opportunities in areas such as space technology, machine learning and engineering.

What I learned: I should not restrict myself to only classroom subjects. Building projects and exploring interdisciplinary areas can reveal new career possibilities.

3. Heloise Hoffmann — Stanford University

Heloise Hoffmann is a Stanford bioengineering student who became involved in research related to facioscapulohumeral muscular dystrophy (FSHD).

She participated in Stanford's iGEM synthetic biology team. The team designed a proposed treatment approach and won a gold medal at the iGEM Grand Jamboree.

She and her teammates later won Stanford's Undergraduate Entrepreneurship Program pitch competition and received $50,000 to continue developing their research project.

Her journey demonstrates the connection between academic knowledge, research, teamwork, competition and entrepreneurship.

What I learned: A student can take an idea from learning, to research, to competition and eventually towards a real-world solution.

4. Tarsh Swarnkar — IIIT Kota

Tarsh Swarnkar is a student of IIIT Kota whose profile demonstrates a strong combination of projects, research, competitions and leadership.

He worked as a Research Intern at DRDO, where he developed a GUI using OpenCV and Tkinter and explored computer vision applications.

He also served as a Placement Coordinator at IIIT Kota.

His team won the Amdocs GenAI Hackathon after competing against more than 11,500 participants from over 300 colleges. Their project was an AI-powered career guidance platform called AI Career Hub.

He was also selected for Amazon ML Summer School 2024.

What I learned: Projects, internships, competitions and leadership can work together to create a strong technical profile.

What I Learned From These Profiles

After studying these students, I understood that a strong student profile is not created only through academic marks.


The common areas I noticed were:

  • Strong academic foundation
  • Continuous learning
  • Technical skills
  • Personal and team projects
  • Research experience
  • Competitions and hackathons
  • Internships
  • Leadership and teamwork
  • Professional networking
  • Documenting achievements and projects

I realized that I should start building my profile gradually instead of waiting until my final year.
